## ZolaNight: Crafted for Speed and Simplicity

After spending countless hours refining my personal website and diving deep into
the capabilities of Zola, I realized a need for a theme that truly resonated
with my aesthetic: minimalist, blazing fast, and highly customizable. This
desire led to the creation of **ZolaNight**.

ZolaNight isn't just another theme; it's a testament to the power of simplicity
and efficiency in web design. Built from the ground up to leverage Zola's
incredible performance, it offers a clean, distraction-free reading experience
without sacrificing modern functionality.

## Design Philosophy

The core principles behind ZolaNight are:

1. **Minimalism:** Focus on content. Unnecessary visual clutter is stripped
   away, allowing your words and ideas to take center stage.
2. **Performance:** Every line of code is optimized for speed. With ZolaNight,
   your site will load almost instantaneously, providing an excellent user
   experience and scoring high on Lighthouse audits.
3. **Readability:** Thoughtful typography and a balanced layout ensure that your
   content is easy on the eyes, encouraging longer reading sessions.
4. **Customization:** While minimalist by default, ZolaNight is designed to be
   easily customizable. From color schemes (inspired by popular editor themes
   like Tokyo Night and Catppuccin) to font choices, you have the flexibility to
   make it your own.

## Key Features

- **Responsive Design:** Looks great on any device, from desktops to mobile
  phones.
- **Syntax Highlighting:** Beautiful code blocks that enhance readability for
  technical content.
- **Configurable Colors:** Easily switch between different color palettes to
  match your brand or personal preference.
- **Lightweight:** Minimal CSS and JavaScript ensure a tiny footprint.

## Getting Started with ZolaNight

Installing ZolaNight is straightforward, especially if you're already familiar
with Zola's theme system. You can include it as a Git submodule in your Zola
project:

```bash
git submodule add https://github.com/mxaddict/zolanight.git themes/zolanight
```

Then, configure your `zola.toml` to use `theme = "zolanight"`. You can also
customize its appearance further by setting parameters under the
`[extra.zolanight]` section.
